What we do

Everyday dentistry, done carefully

From a routine check‑up to a crown, a whitening course or an unexpected toothache — almost everything happens here, with people you know.

Check-ups & hygiene

Thorough examinations, x‑rays where needed, and 30‑minute appointments with our in‑house hygienist to keep gums healthy.

Exams from £55

Emergencies

There is nothing quite like toothache. We aim to see all new patients the same day, within our working hours, and get you comfortable quickly.

Same-day help

Tooth whitening

Professional home whitening with custom trays. Most people see a difference within a week, with the full course taking four to five weeks.

Upper & lower £350

Crowns, bridges & veneers

Bonded porcelain crowns and veneers to restore a damaged tooth or improve the look of a smile, matched to your natural shade.

From £610

Fillings, root canals & dentures

Straightforward restorative work, root treatments to save a tooth, and well‑fitting dentures made to measure.

Why patients stay

The same people, appointment after appointment

The practice has been established for over 50 years, and we are delighted to continue to build on its strong reputation for patient centred care — tucked between Elm Grove, Albert Road and Palmerston Road.

What patients tell us they value most is continuity. You are not passed between locums. You see a dentist who remembers your history, your worries and what worked last time — and a reception team who know your name when you walk in.

Meet the team What to expect

Gentle with anxious patients

Many of our patients arrived nervous, often after years away. We go at your pace, explain each step, and stop whenever you need us to.
