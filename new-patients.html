Welcoming new patients

New patients

Joining a new dental practice should not be complicated. Here is exactly what happens, what it costs, and what we will ask you.

Your first visit

Four steps, no surprises

1. Get in touch

Ring the practice or send an enquiry. Reception will find a time that works and tell you what to expect.

2. Tell us your history

We will ask about your medical history, any medication you take, and what has or has not gone well at the dentist before.

3. A full examination

A thorough look at teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues, with two x‑rays included in the new patient fee of £88.

4. A written plan

If anything needs doing, you get the options and the costs in writing before you commit to anything.

If you are nervous

It is fine to be anxious. Say so.

A large number of the people we see arrive apologising for having stayed away for years. There is nothing to apologise for, and no lecture coming.

Many of our patients are very fearful of the dentist, and often come to their first appointment needing emergency treatment. We do all we can to relax them and provide a gentle, friendly service. Often they then become regular patients — some travelling a considerable distance to see us.

Tell us when you book that you are anxious, and we will take it into account. We aim to treat every patient as we would like to be treated ourselves.

  • A gentle, friendly service for nervous patients
  • Everything explained before it happens
  • No judgement about how long it has been

Second opinions

If another practice has recommended extensive or expensive work and you would like another view, you are very welcome to come and talk it through. Bring any treatment plan, estimate or recent x‑rays if you have them.

A consultation carries the standard new patient examination fee; there is no obligation to transfer your care to us afterwards.

The practicalities

NHS, private and Denplan

Adults — private

At the practice we offer private adult dentistry. Our full price list is published, and you will have a written estimate before treatment.

See fees

Children — NHS when available

We can see children under 18 on the NHS when space is available. When it is not, our private children’s fees start at £35 for a routine examination.

Children’s fees

Pay as you go, or Denplan

Pay for each visit as it comes, or join Denplan Essentials: monthly instalments covering examinations and hygiene, 10% off treatment, and worldwide emergency cover.

About Denplan

Please bring with you: a list of any medicines you take, details of any medical conditions or allergies, your previous dentist’s details if you would like your records transferred, and a form of ID if you are registering a child.

Ready to book, or just want to ask?

Whether you are due a check‑up, in pain today, or thinking about whitening or a crown, we are happy to talk it through first. No pressure, no jargon.
