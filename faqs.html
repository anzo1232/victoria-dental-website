Questions & advice

The things patients ask us most often — about joining the practice, about cost, and about looking after your teeth between visits.

How do I register as a new patient?

Call the practice on 023 9281 1493 or send us an enquiry through the contact page. Reception will take a few details and book you a new patient examination. There is no waiting list for private adult care.

Do you take NHS patients?

We are able to see children on the NHS when space is available. Adult care at the practice is private, with Denplan payment plans available for those who would rather budget monthly.

What does a first appointment cost?

A new patient examination is £88 and includes two x‑rays. Routine six‑month examinations afterwards are £55. For children the private new patient fee is £37. The full price list is published on this site.

Can you see me today if I am in pain?

We aim to see all new patients the same day for emergencies, within our working hours. Ring as early in the day as you can — emergency slots fill quickly. See dental emergencies for what to do in the meantime.

How many times a day should I brush?

It is important to brush at least twice a day using a soft toothbrush and a fluoride toothpaste. Two minutes each time, last thing at night and on one other occasion, does more for your teeth than anything else you can buy.

Why a soft toothbrush?

A soft toothbrush makes it much easier to remove the plaque below the gum line, which helps prevent periodontal (gum) disease. Hard bristles feel more thorough but tend to abrade enamel and drive gums back rather than cleaning under them.

Why fluoride toothpaste?

Fluoride hardens the outer enamel layer of the teeth and helps them resist decay. Spit after brushing, but do not rinse with water — rinsing washes away the fluoride before it has had a chance to work.

What causes bad breath, and what helps?

Around 96% of adults experience bad breath at some point. The usual causes are a build‑up of bacteria from incomplete cleaning, certain foods such as garlic and onions, not drinking enough water, gum disease, illness, hormonal changes, stress, sinus problems, and simple morning breath.

What helps:

  • Brush twice a day and floss or use interdental brushes daily
  • Clean your tongue — a great deal of the smell originates there
  • Drink water regularly and eat at regular intervals
  • Use a good quality mouthwash, and an antibacterial toothpaste
  • Clean dentures properly and leave them out overnight
  • See a dentist regularly, since persistent bad breath usually has a treatable cause
How long does tooth whitening take?

Our home whitening system uses custom‑made trays and professional gel. Most people see a visible difference after about a week, and the full course typically takes four to five weeks. Upper and lower whitening is £350.

Do you offer orthodontics or braces?

We refer patients who need orthodontic treatment to a trusted local orthodontist rather than providing it in‑house. Ask at your examination and we will arrange the referral.

I have not been to a dentist in years. Will I get a telling off?

No. A lot of our patients came back after a long gap, often because something started hurting. We are interested in what we can do from here, not in what did not happen before. Tell us you are nervous when you book and we will allow extra time.

Where can I park?

There is plenty of free residential parking behind the practice in Outram Road and Campbell Road. The no. 2 bus between Portsmouth and Eastney stops on Victoria Road North, and Fratton railway station is a short walk away.

What happens to my personal information?

The practice complies with the Data Protection Act and UK GDPR. Your records are kept confidential and are only shared where you have agreed or where the law requires it. See our privacy notice for the detail.

Not answered here? Ring the practice on 023 9281 1493 or send us a question. Reception is happy to answer general queries without an appointment, and a telephone consultation with a dentist is £45 where clinical advice is needed.

Ready to book, or just want to ask?

Whether you are due a check‑up, in pain today, or thinking about whitening or a crown, we are happy to talk it through first. No pressure, no jargon.
