Tooth whitening

Over time your teeth pick up stains and discolouration. Wine, coffee, tea and smoking all contribute to the natural ageing process that darkens teeth. We use a professional home‑whitening system: custom trays and gel that you wear at home. Most people notice a difference within a week, with the full course taking around four to five weeks.

Paying for treatment

Pay as you go, or Denplan Essentials

You can simply pay for each visit as it comes — or join Denplan Essentials and spread the cost.

Denplan Essentials lets you pay for your examinations and hygiene appointments in monthly instalments, with the added benefit of a 10% reduction on treatment costs. It also has built‑in insurance that covers you for emergency treatment anywhere in the world.

Ask at reception and we will explain which option fits how often you actually need to be seen.

Ask about Denplan Denplan website

A written estimate, every time

Before treatment starts you get the plan and the cost in writing, so there is nothing to work out afterwards.

Options, not ultimatums

Where there is more than one sensible way to treat something, we will set out each option with its cost and its trade‑offs.

Referrals when they are right

We do not have an orthodontist on site, but we can refer you to an excellent local orthodontist whose practice is only a few doors away from us.
