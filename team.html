Behind the surgery door

Nurses, hygienist and reception

A dental practice is far more than its dentists. Ours runs on a hygienist, a team of qualified nurses and a reception team who between them have given this practice decades of service.

That continuity is the whole point. It is why you are greeted by name, why nobody has to re‑read your notes from scratch, and why patients who moved away from Southsea years ago still make the trip back.

All of our nurses and hygienists are registered with the General Dental Council and undertake continuing professional development every year.
